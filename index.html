The best GTA 6 moments, ready for Shorts

GTA6 Clips is a Windows desktop app that turns Twitch gaming clips into captioned vertical videos and posts them to the creator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Find the hype

Picks popular GTA 6 clips from Twitch where streamers are talking about the game.

Make it vertical

Reframes each clip to 1080×1920 and adds bold word-by-word captions.

Credit the streamer

Every post names and links the original Twitch streamer.

Post to your account

Connect your own TikTok, then post with one click or on a daily schedule.
