Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

GTA6 Clips is a Windows desktop app that turns Twitch gaming clips into vertical videos and posts them to the user's own TikTok account. This policy explains what information it uses.

Information we access

When you connect a TikTok account, GTA6 Clips receives from TikTok:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar), through the user.info.basic permission.
  • Permission to post videos to your profile, through the video.publish permission.
  • Permission to send videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts, through the video.upload permission.

How information is used

This information is used only to show which account is connected and to post the videos you chose or scheduled to that account. GTA6 Clips does not read your other videos, followers, messages or activity.

Storage

Login tokens are stored only on your own computer. GTA6 Clips has no server and does not send account information anywhere except to TikTok's official API.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties.

Removing access

You can remove access at any time by clicking Disconnect in the app, or by removing GTA6 Clips under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services in TikTok. Disconnecting deletes the stored login from your computer.

Contact

Questions can be sent by message to @gta6clipper71 on TikTok.
